## Choose an online or local model

- **Online models** require a network connection and your own API credentials. Audio is sent to the selected provider.
- **Local models** require an initial download, then recognize audio on your device. No API key is needed, but they use local memory and processing resources. See [Local speech recognition models](/en/docs/local-models) for downloads and inference settings.

Compare languages, hotwords, and streaming in the [compatibility list](/en/docs/compatibility).

## Online models

1. Open **Model Services** in the sidebar and expand a provider. Use **Show remaining** at the bottom if it is not listed.
2. For a provider with multiple capabilities, select **Speech recognition**, then fill in **Credentials**. Guides: [Volcengine](/en/docs/api/volcengine), [ElevenLabs](/en/docs/api/elevenlabs), [Qwen](/en/docs/api/qwen).
3. Click a model row to open **Model configuration**, keep the defaults, and click **Test**. Use **Add model** to restore a removed preset.
4. After a successful test, click **Set as default**, or select the model under **Speech recognition** at the top of Model Services.
5. Record a sentence in a text field with a shortcut to check actual recording and pasting.

## Model tests

**Test** sends a built-in audio sample to the selected recognizer without switching the current model. Online tests send the sample to the provider and may count toward service usage; local tests run on your device.

**Model request succeeded** confirms that sample request. It does not check microphone permissions, shortcuts, or auto-paste. Finish setup by trying real voice input in another app.

## Custom configuration

Click the gear beside **Providers** in Model Services to open **Custom Configuration** for shared recognition settings. Keep defaults unless needed; effects depend on the model and inference backend. Click an individual model row for its own parameters.

### Audio sampling

- **Sample rate:** the number of audio samples captured per second. Check that the model or service supports the rate before changing it.
- **Channel:** the number of audio channels. Keep the default unless the model or service requires otherwise.

### Recognition enhancement

- **Hotword boost:** chooses whether to send hotwords to the polishing model: auto, off, or on. It applies when polishing is used; see [Hotwords](/en/docs/hotwords).
- **Hotword replacement:** restores recognized hotwords to their saved spelling and format.
