---
title: "System permissions"
description: "Grant microphone access on macOS and Windows, plus macOS Accessibility permission, so VoicePaste can record your voice and paste text into other apps."
---

## macOS

### Microphone

1. In VoicePaste, open Settings → General settings → Permissions.
2. Use the microphone check button and allow access in the system prompt.
3. Return to VoicePaste settings, check again, and confirm a checkmark appears beside the permission.

### Accessibility

Accessibility permission lets VoicePaste enter text into other apps.

1. In Settings → General settings → Permissions → Accessibility, use the button to open system settings.
2. Find VoicePaste under Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on its switch.
3. Return to VoicePaste settings, check again, and confirm a checkmark appears beside the permission.

## Windows

### Microphone

1. Search for “microphone” in Windows Settings, open microphone privacy settings, and allow microphone access for apps and desktop apps.
2. Return to VoicePaste and use the microphone check button under System Permissions.
